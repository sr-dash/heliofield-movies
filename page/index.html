The Sun since 2010, one rotation per frame

Each frame is one Carrington rotation, about 27 days: SDO's AIA channels and HMI magnetogram, and STEREO-A's EUVI 195 Å, as stored on the HelioField Observations page. The field-line versions add HelioField's PFSS model from HMI rotation maps, seen from each spacecraft. The sunspot curve follows the solar cycle through the frames.

STEREO-A has gaps where it was behind the Sun as seen from Earth (2014–2015) and on the few days its beacon sent another channel than 195 Å, and the first rotation in May 2010 predates AIA's synoptic images.
